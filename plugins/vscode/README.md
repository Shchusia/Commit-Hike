# Commit Hike

Every commit you make moves you along a hiking trail, right inside VS Code.
A day of steady work takes you about ten kilometers along a real mountain
ridge, a desert crossing or a folk tale, with a scene that changes as you go,
a map, stories, facts and achievements.

*In your language: [English](https://github.com/Shchusia/Commit-Hike/blob/master/README.md) · [Українська](https://github.com/Shchusia/Commit-Hike/blob/master/README.uk.md) · [Polski](https://github.com/Shchusia/Commit-Hike/blob/master/README.pl.md) · [Deutsch](https://github.com/Shchusia/Commit-Hike/blob/master/README.de.md) · [Español](https://github.com/Shchusia/Commit-Hike/blob/master/README.es.md)*

- **Real trails.** The Chornohora ridge over Hoverla, the Tongariro Alpine Crossing, the Tour du Mont Blanc and the Camino Francés on their real GPS tracks; Santiago's road from The Alchemist across real Spain, Morocco and the Sahara; folk tales and original stories. Ten routes, in two series you can walk one after another.
- **A hike you can watch.** A side-on scene that follows the route's real elevation, the weather, the season and your clock. Look back along the way you came; what lies ahead stays hidden until you get there.
- **A real map.** A topographic map of the whole trail: zoom in like on paper, the scale bar follows and more names appear. A minimap in the scene.
- **Real distances, your difficulty.** A typical day of commits is about 10 km on medium (12.5 on easy, 8 on hard), so a long route is a goal for months.
- **Stories, facts and achievements** at every stop, streaks, days off, your year in a calendar and a passport with a stamp for every stop.
- **Share it.** A postcard of where you are, saved, copied or posted to X, Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, LinkedIn, Facebook, Telegram or Reddit with the text ready. And an SVG badge for your GitHub profile.
- **Walk together.** Everyone committing to a project on the same trail, with a leaderboard, the team's week and a team goal: a route you walk together, everyone's commits adding up.
- **In the terminal too.** The same progress in your shell prompt and in Neovim: [docs/terminal.md](https://github.com/Shchusia/Commit-Hike/blob/master/docs/terminal.md).
- **Your own routes.** Create one from a template and import it as a folder or a `.zip`.
- **Five languages.** English, Ukrainian, Polish, German and Spanish.

## Private by design

- **Nothing touches your projects.** No git hooks, no files in your repositories: Commit Hike only reads your git history.
- **Nothing leaves your computer.** No accounts, no telemetry. The only web pages it opens are the sharing pages you pick in the Share menu.
- **Real work counts.** Lock files, generated code, whitespace-only changes and other people's commits don't.

## Getting started

Open the **Commit Hike** view in the activity bar. A short setup asks which
projects count and where your journey starts. Then just commit.

All commands are in the command palette under *Commit Hike*: choosing a trail,
importing your own route, the hiker icon, difficulty, language and more.
