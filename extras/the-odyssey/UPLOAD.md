## Description (paste as is: it's Markdown)

**Homer's voyage home**, from Troy to Ithaca, on a real map of the Mediterranean: about **4,900 km** of sea.

The Lotus-eaters, the Cyclops, the bag of winds, Circe's island, the Sirens, Scylla and Charybdis, seven years with Calypso, and an old dog that knows his master at once. 15 stops on the places people have identified with Homer's since antiquity, 6 real facts, 3 dangers on the way.

*Мандрівка Одіссея додому — від Трої до Ітаки на справжній мапі Середземномор'я. Сирени, циклоп, Скілла й Харибда. Тексти англійською та українською.*

Where Homer's places really were is argued about to this day; the stops follow traditional identifications. The poem is about 2,800 years old and free for everyone.
