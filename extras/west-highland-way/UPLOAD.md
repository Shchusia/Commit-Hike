## Description (paste as is: it's Markdown)

Scotland's best-loved long-distance trail, **154 km** from the edge of Glasgow to Fort William.

Along the bonnie banks of **Loch Lomond**, over the Highland Boundary Fault at Conic Hill, across the empty bog of **Rannoch Moor**, up the **Devil's Staircase** out of Glen Coe and down to the foot of **Ben Nevis**, the highest mountain in Britain. 15 stops, 5 facts, red deer and midges included.
