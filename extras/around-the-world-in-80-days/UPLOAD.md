## Description (paste as is: it's Markdown)

**Phileas Fogg's wager** from Jules Verne's novel of 1872: round the world and back to the Reform Club in eighty days.

London, Paris, the new tunnel under the Alps, **Suez**, Aden, **Bombay**, a railway that stops in the jungle and an elephant, **Calcutta**, Singapore, **Hong Kong**, a typhoon off Shanghai, **Yokohama**, the 180th meridian, **San Francisco**, the Pacific railroad over the Rockies, an attack on the prairie, **New York**, a steamer burned for fuel in the Atlantic, Liverpool and home. 25 stops, 6 real facts, 3 dangers.

**The world is shrunk to 800 km** so that about **80 typical days of commits** take you round, like Fogg. The stops keep their real proportions, on a real world map (Natural Earth) laid out as a strip: London at both ends, and the day you gain going east is waiting in mid-Pacific.

*Парі Філеаса Фоґґа за романом Жуля Верна: довкола світу за вісімдесят днів. Світ стиснуто до 800 км, щоб близько 80 звичайних днів комітів провели тебе довкола. Тексти англійською та українською.*

The novel is in the public domain.
